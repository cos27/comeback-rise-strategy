1. CORE BRAND POSITIONING

Comeback Operating System™ (COS) is an AI-powered career comeback brand helping women Restart, Rebuild & Rise after a career break.

Core tagline:

“Empowering Women to Restart, Rebuild & Rise — Your AI-Powered Career Comeback Partner.”

Core belief:

“No woman should give up her career because she took a break.”

COS combines:

- Career strategy

- Practical career guidance

- Professional branding

- AI career readiness

- Resume and LinkedIn support

- Interview preparation

- Personalized career guidance

The website should feel like a premium career transformation brand, not a generic coaching website and not an AI software company.

2. TARGET AUDIENCE

Design primarily for women who:

- Are returning to work after a career break

- Are planning to restart their careers

- Are currently job searching after a break

- Have lost confidence in their professional identity

- Are unsure how to explain their career gap

- Need help updating their resume

- Need help rebuilding their LinkedIn presence

- Want to become AI-ready

- Need interview preparation

- Want clarity about their next career move

The emotional reaction we want from the visitor is:

“This understands exactly where I am, and there is a clear path forward.”
